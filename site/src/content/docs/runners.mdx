The repository's [receipt experiment report](https://github.com/agentcourses/agentcourses/blob/main/docs/delivery/call-receipts.md#host-evidence) inspected host interfaces. Its synthetic tests do not demonstrate a production receipt boundary. The [approved capability contract](https://github.com/agentcourses/agentcourses/blob/main/docs/delivery/call-receipts.md#approved-capability-contract--19-october-2026) requires separate qualification for every host. Stock prompt labels, callbacks and tutor-reachable signing tools do not establish learner provenance.

## A declaration needs evidence

The candidate recognises only `run-checks`, `observe` and `judge`. A declaration says what a runner claims to do; evidence must show that behaviour on each named host. No capability badges appear on these planned tiles because the candidate establishes none of them for Tutr. See [capabilities](/specification/#execution--capabilities) and the [conformance guide](/implementers/conformance/) for their distinct evidence requirements.

A check passing is not proof that the learner owned a decision. Without the [receipt boundary](/specification/#records--receipt-boundary), learner calls remain unverified and cannot support settlement of objectives that depend on them. A runner with no capabilities can still deliver lessons, but settles no objective. Candidate review qualifies neither a host nor a stable runner ([candidate and stable](/specification/#records--candidate-and-stable)).

## Before claiming support

A future listing should identify the implementation release, target format version, tested host and declared capabilities. It should link reproducible runtime evidence and disclose missing capabilities and receipt limits. The internal checkout reader checks current-format courses and controlled replay fixtures. The [support guide](/implementers/support/) explains the work; the [conformance guide](/implementers/conformance/) separates current repository evidence from the future public suite. This page lists plans only, with no released package or independent runner implied.
